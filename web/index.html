QuboBoard

A useful-work marketplace on QMS Testnet. Clients post QUBO optimization jobs with a reward escrowed in QMS; solvers submit solution hashes with their objective value (lower is better); the client accepts one solution and the reward is paid on-chain. Built for the QMS Builder Program, Cohort 1.

Contract: · Explorer · Faucet (test QMS)

⚠️ No contract address is configured yet (see web/config.js). The app is read-only until the contract is deployed and its address is set.

Post a job

The reward is held by the contract until you accept a solution, or refunded in full if you cancel.

Jobs

Newest first. Solutions are ordered by submission time; compare objective values yourself before accepting.

Submit a solution

The app hashes your assignment with keccak256 and stores only the hash + objective on-chain, so your solution stays private until you choose to reveal it.
